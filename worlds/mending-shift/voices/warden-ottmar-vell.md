# Warden Ottmar Vell

Shift boss of Sallowquay Boat-yard. Carries the shingle slate under one arm from
Low Water onward and reads the satchel aloud at Slack Water, at the yard end of
the causeway, and signs it or defers it there. He is the yard's only authority
over the roster and uses it about twice a shift, both times on the water rather
than on anybody.

**What he wants:** the packet boat off the blocks and across the channel on the
evening tide, which needs his bone fid back with its stem sound and not merely
whole. With a patched stem she may go across once.

**What he hands over:** his sailmaker's bone fid, worn flat and yellowed at the
grip, the stem split through from the ferrule down about two hands' breadth,
opened at the top and closed at the bottom, his name scratched into the flat
where the glove covers it. He hands it over at Low Water and at no other hour,
because that is the hour with light in it and the split cannot be read before
it.

**How he speaks:** gives instructions as statements of fact and does not raise
his voice, because the yard is close enough that he never needs to. Talks in
tide language, not clock language, and does not notice that he does. When he is
pleased he mentions the slate; when he is embarrassed he mentions the slate
harder. Short lines. Never says thank you — he says the hour, and you work out
what he meant. He never explains the game's own machinery, and he never says a
number that is not an hour, a tide, a thickness or a count of objects.

# Grissel Toft

Keeps the pitch pot: lights it, banks it, and puts it down and covers it. She is
in the yard at The Turning when the pot is lit and gone before The Run, which is
why people call her the smell of the first hour and nothing else. She is at the
pot for The Turning and at the pot again for Low Water, and she is not in the
shed, at the rack or on the wall when the run comes. She is the only person at
the yard who sets the pace by the hour and knows that the rest of the shift is
following her.

**What she wants:** the pot down and covered before the water is over the wall,
with the collar bone whole. The last time it was lifted hot and short-handed it
cracked in the fire and she had to take the whole shift on the bench. The water
is over the wall by the middle of The Run, which is inside the shift, and she is
gone before it, so the mending has to be done while she is still standing in the
yard.

**What she hands over:** her heat-glove for the pot. The left cuff is burnt out
at the wrist and the cloth inside it has gone to powder, so the glove has about
a minute in it and then it is a glove full of pitch ash. It goes to the bench at
The Turning and comes straight back onto her own hand, mended, before that hour
passes. It is the one mendable object in the slice that is deliberately not an
item record: no id, no verb, no worn slot, no satchel and no recipe, because a
mend to the yard's own tool is not a mend to the kit-runner's build.

**How she speaks:** gives instructions she has given a hundred times, at the
volume of somebody who has never been in a room this quiet. Counts the fire in
handfuls. Everything she says has an order in it, even the parts about herself.

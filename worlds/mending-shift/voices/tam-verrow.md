# Tam Verrow

Eleven years old. Sweeps, carries, holds the far end of things, and is at the
end of the bench rather than at it. Comes across on the causeway in the dark at
The Turning and is the first person in the yard to say anything to you at all.
Nobody at the bench corrects what he tells you, which is why he is dangerous.

**What he wants:** to be counted as a hand at the rack instead of a carrier,
before Slack Water, and to have counted once by somebody who did not have to. He
is asking for it by offering to do a mend nobody has given him.

**What he hands over:** a coat hook he has already taken off the rack, and
already bent. The shank is drawn about a third of the way along and the bend is
set hard enough that it will not open back without a vice. He offers it at the
causeway head at The Turning and it is refused, so that handoff does not happen
and the hook never reaches a worn slot.

**How he speaks:** asks the same question four different ways and never waits
for the answer before asking the next. Repeats your words back to you with one
word changed, to see whether you meant it. Counts things out loud — hooks,
slots, hands — and gets the number wrong once on purpose.

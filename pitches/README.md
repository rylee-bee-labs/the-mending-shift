# M0 pitches, side by side

**All three are MOCKUP pitches and not canon until Rylee picks one.** No build, no playtest and no spend exist
against any of them; no playtest has been run for any of the three and none is claimed.

## The three compared

Pitch A, *The Mending Shift*, is a tide-set boat-yard craft game in which a kit-runner gains capability by repairing
and mending other people's tools, and it is built on A1 Rank 1, build assembly. Pitch B, *Slackwater Shift*, is a
night-shift freight-route game about deciding how often to stop and how fast to clear each interruption, and it is
built on A1 Rank 2, encounter rhythm. Pitch C, *Plain Speaking*, is a small-town mystery in which the game states its
own deepest rule aloud, in the fiction, every time it fires, and it is built on A1 Rank 3, legibility. The three
separate on tone (bench craft, schedule-driven administration, plain-spoken deduction), on setting (a tidal boat-yard,
a night freight line, the invented salt town of Saltmere) and on which ranked mechanic each one takes as its
signature.

| Pitch | Title | Signature mechanic (A1 rank) | Tone | Feasibility risk | Slice length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | The Mending Shift | Kitting: a carried object grants or rewrites a verb, and fusing two pieces mends one (build assembly, Rank 1) | Bench craft on a tide-set boat-yard floor | MISSING: no wait or rest verb and a free phase jump, so time cannot cost anything | The full hour, 4am to slack water |
| B | Slackwater Shift | The Shift Ledger: filing, cadence and line decide how often you are interrupted and how fast it resolves (encounter rhythm, Rank 2) | Administrative, schedule-driven night work | MISSING: no turn system, so contact resolution is hand-authored content | Ninety minutes of night, four yards |
| C | Plain Speaking | The game states its own deepest rule aloud, in the fiction, each time it fires (legibility, Rank 3) | Plain-spoken, rule-stating small-town mystery | MISSING: no phase condition in the rules vocabulary, so legibility is authoring discipline | The full hour, dawn to small hours |

## Where each cell comes from

Titles, mechanics and slice timings are taken verbatim from each pitch file. The signature mechanic of each pitch maps
to one of the three mechanics ranked in `research/A1-mechanics.md`: Rank 1 build assembly, Rank 2 encounter rhythm,
Rank 3 legibility. Each feasibility risk is the largest dependency that pitch's own "VEFR feasibility" section states
as PARTIAL or MISSING against `research/A2-feasibility.md`; the full BUILT / PACK-ONLY / PARTIAL / MISSING breakdown,
with `file:line` for each, stays in the pitch file itself.

Each pitch's costs are stated in its own "Scope and risks" section, and each one describes its slice as needing no
engine change as scoped. The choice among A, B and C is Rylee's; this sheet makes no argument for or against any of
them.

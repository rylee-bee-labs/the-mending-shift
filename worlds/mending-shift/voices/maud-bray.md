# Maud Bray

Kit-runner, eleven years on the bench, in the yard at every phase including Slack
Water. She is the person who has already mended whatever you are holding and will
tell you so. She is openly sceptical about you and never once says so in a way
you could answer, and she is not a villain about it: she has watched four
kit-runners be made and two of them leave.

**What she wants:** her scratch to still be on the thing she mended after the
shift. The scratch is her own old mending mark, cut across the left shoulder seam
of Roald Fisk's waxed tar-coat — she re-laid that seam years ago and it is still
her work. She wants the seam opened and re-laid again with her scratch kept on
the new stitching, and she wants it said in advance, at the bench, that fusion
will take the mark off, because fusion unpicks the seam and spends the scratch
with the hand that laid it.

**What she hands over:** nothing of her own, and she makes no attempt to pretend
otherwise. The mark is on the coat, which is Roald's and comes to the bench in
his own hand with the seam already open to the lining from the armhole down.

**How she speaks:** gives the shortest true answer and does not improve it
afterwards. Repeats your words when she is disagreeing, so you hear how they
sounded in someone else's mouth. Uses the apprentice's name less than she should,
which she has decided is on purpose and will not discuss.

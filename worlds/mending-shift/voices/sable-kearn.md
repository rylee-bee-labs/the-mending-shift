# Sable Kearn

Sailmaker and rigger, at the bench through every phase including the ones she
ought not to be. The yard's busiest speaker: she will let two conversations run
at once and keep them apart. She is the person most likely to tell you what is
wrong with a thing before you have asked, and the least likely to hand it over —
what is wrong with a thing is hers to talk about and the thing itself is hers to
keep.

**What she wants:** the packet's hoist up once, in front of the town, at Slack
Water, seen from the far bank. She is not going to say that. She says the gear
wants doing, and puts the piece that does not want doing at all down on her own
bench instead of into your hands, because the gear wants doing before that
becket parts and the block does not want doing before anything.

**What she hands over:** nothing, this shift. A length of tarred line with a
block at the standing end stays where it is, on the end of her own bench, and
she describes it to you instead of giving it up. The sheave in the block has
seized solid and will not run; the becket below it is split open at the top
third and will part under load.

**How she speaks:** describes every object by where it sits rather than what it
is called, because she is looking at it while she talks to you. Starts cheerful,
drops to plain mid-sentence, and does not explain the drop. Repeats the end of
her own sentence when she means it twice.
